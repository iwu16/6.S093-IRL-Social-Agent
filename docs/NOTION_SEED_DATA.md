## Page 1: Brand Voice Guide

### Who We Are

IRL is a mobile app for joining small, real-world activities happening right now in your city.

### Voice Principles

**Calm, not urgent**
We don't create FOMO. We create possibility. There's always another walk, another coffee, another chance.

**Human, not corporate**
We write like a thoughtful friend texts, not like a brand broadcasts. Short sentences. Real words.

**Understated, not hype**
We never say "amazing" or "incredible." The best things in life don't need adjectives.

**Grounded, not digital**
We talk about real places, real weather, real moments. Not "experiences" or "connections."

### Words We Use
- walk, coffee, study session
- join, go, meet
- nearby, right now, today
- simple, small, real

### Words We Avoid
- revolutionary, game-changing, incredible
- network, connect (as verbs)
- experience (as a noun)
- community (overused)
- any emoji

## Page 2: Company Mission

### Why IRL Exists

People are lonelier than ever, but not because they lack friends. They lack *moments*. Spontaneous, unplanned, low-pressure moments where real connection happens.

IRL exists to lower the barrier for those moments. Not to create a social network. Not to build communities. Just to help someone say "I'm going for a walk, anyone want to join?" and have it actually work.

### What Success Looks Like

- Someone joins a stranger's coffee break and makes a friend
- A neighborhood walk becomes a weekly ritual
- People feel less alone without having to "put themselves out there"

### What We're Not

- Not a dating app
- Not a professional networking tool
- Not an events platform
- Not trying to maximize engagement

## Page 3: Product Principles

### Core Beliefs

1. **Small is better than big**
   A 3-person walk beats a 30-person meetup. Intimacy matters.

2. **Now is better than later**
   We focus on what's happening in the next few hours, not next week.

3. **Showing up is enough**
   You don't need to be interesting. You just need to be there.

4. **Real places ground us**
   We use real locations, real neighborhoods. Not virtual anything.

### Anti-Features

Things we deliberately don't build:
- Follower counts or social graphs
- Public profiles with bios
- Event RSVPs or commitments
- Algorithmic feeds
- Notifications designed to create anxiety

## Page 4: Target Users

### Who Uses IRL

**The Quietly Lonely**
People with friends but not enough spontaneous hang time. Working from home. New to a city. Just... less connected than they used to be.

**The Over-Scheduled**
People whose social lives require too much planning. They want low-effort ways to see humans.

**The Curious Introvert**
People who want to meet new people but hate networking events and forced social situations.

### What They Want

- Something to do right now, not next weekend
- Low commitment, easy to bail
- Not performative or public
- Feels natural, not "organized"

## Page 5: Content Themes

### Topics We Talk About

1. **Spontaneity**
   The value of unplanned moments. Why the best memories aren't scheduled.

2. **Small Interactions**
   A nod to a neighbor. A "nice day" to a stranger. How tiny moments add up.

3. **Being Present**
   Getting off the phone. Noticing your neighborhood. Slowing down.

4. **Loneliness (Gently)**
   Acknowledging that lots of people feel disconnected. No shame. No pressure.

5. **Simple Activities**
   Walking. Coffee. Reading at a park. Study sessions. Nothing fancy.

### Topics We Avoid

- Productivity culture
- Self-improvement language
- Wellness trends
- Influencer aesthetics
- Anything that feels like marketing
